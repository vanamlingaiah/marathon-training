PREP DAY · Clear Liquids
⚠️ Prep day rules
No structured workout. Hydrate aggressively. Follow timing strictly.
Today is logistics, not training. Skipping the workout is the correct call — you can't fuel properly, will be calorie-deprived all afternoon, and bowel prep evening makes any session miserable.
🍎 Light breakfast · finish by 8 AM (latest 10 AM)
Light on stomach — avoid heavy fiber today (counterintuitive, but you want the colon clearing easily). Good options: scrambled eggs + plain white toast + small banana. Skip the overnight oats and high-fiber stuff today only.
💧 10 AM onward · Clear liquid diet
Aim for 80–100 oz total liquid through the day — far more than a normal day. Mix between:
• Water · 30 oz
• Gatorade / Powerade · 32 oz (sodium + carbs critical)
• Clear broth · 16 oz at lunch and dinner times
• Apple juice or white grape juice · 16 oz
• Coffee (no milk/cream) · OK in moderation
Avoid: all red, orange, purple liquids/jellos — they look like blood during the procedure.
🚶 Optional · Easy walk only 20 min
Late morning if you feel up to it. Around the neighborhood, slow pace. Helps mental focus and prevents stiffness from a sedentary day. Do NOT bike or run.
⏰ 7:00 PM · Anti-nausea pill
Take one Zofran/Ondansetron tablet 1 hour before prep starts. This makes the prep much more tolerable. Don't skip this step.
Pro tip
Chill the prep solution in the fridge for 2 hours before drinking — much easier to get down cold. Use a straw, drink fast, suck on a lemon wedge or hard candy (clear) between glasses to mask the taste.
⏰ 8:00 PM · First half of prep
Drink first half of prep solution. Then 16 oz water minimum before 10 PM. Expect bowel movements 1–3 hours later — they'll continue until clear yellow liquid. Stay near the bathroom.
🛌 Pre-bed · 10:30 PM
Sleep early — Thursday is a 3:30 AM start for second prep dose. Set TWO alarms. Lay out comfortable clothes for tomorrow (loose fit, easy on/off). Place water bottle and second prep dose by the bed.

First hour back: water, broth, crackers. Hours 2–4: normal but light meal — soup, plain rice, eggs, banana. Avoid heavy meals, alcohol, raw vegetables, or anything spicy for 24 hours.
🛌 Afternoon + Evening · Total rest
No driving, no work decisions, no exercise. Sedation effects can last 12–24 hours even if you feel fine. Nap, watch TV, hydrate. Take notes on whatever doctor told you — sedation can affect memory of post-procedure conversation.
