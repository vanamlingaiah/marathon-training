💪

Strength rule for Week 21: "5 before you clip in"

Two weeks, zero strength sessions. The new rule is simple: do 5 glute exercises before you touch the bike. No Zwift until those 5 are done. Takes 12 minutes flat. The exercises are written on the Monday card. Same 5 repeat on Thursday. This is not optional — every skipped strength week compounds PFPS and back risk.

💧

HR strap fix for Zwift sessions

Wednesday's avg HR 101 on a 52-min ride was a sensor dropout (max still hit 143). Fix: wet both electrode pads on your chest strap with water or conductive gel before every Zwift session. A dry strap loses signal in the first 10–15 minutes of riding, skewing the average permanently for that session.
