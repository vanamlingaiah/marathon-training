You did the right thing

A full week off was the correct response — pushing through would have prolonged the issue and risked injury elsewhere from compensation patterns. Fiber + hydration is exactly the right protocol, and it's working. Keep both going through this week — recovery is not "done" the moment symptoms ease.

📉

What 7 days off actually costs you

Aerobic capacity drops about 5–7% after 7 days of zero training, but the good news: most of it returns within 10–14 days of resuming. The real risk this week is doing too much too soon. Even if you feel ready for a 60-min run on Tuesday — don't. The cardiovascular system rebounds in days; tendons, fascia, and the gut take longer. Stick to the plan even if you feel great.

The optical HR is the immediate bridge if you're still deciding between the HRM-600 and H10.

Health caveats — still in recovery mode

"Feeling better" ≠ "fully healed." This week: still no squats, lunges, step-ups, or heavy bearing-down movements. Still wide saddle / padded shorts for bike if any saddle discomfort. Still high-fiber + 80+ oz water daily. Symptoms can flare from over-doing it — stop any session immediately if anything feels wrong.

✈️ Travel PM — practical tips
Walk through the airport — don't take moving walkways or elevators if you can avoid it.
Stand and stretch every hour on the plane. Calf raises in your seat × 15 every 30 min — venous flush + hemorrhoid pressure relief.
Hydrate aggressively — 16 oz water before flight, 8 oz per hour in the air. Skip airplane coffee/alcohol entirely.
