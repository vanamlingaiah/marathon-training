Running is the priority now — 3 runs, spaced out

With Houston 17 weeks out, running moves to the center: Monday, Wednesday, and the Saturday long run. They're deliberately spaced so you're never running two days back-to-back — your standing injury rule, and the thing that protects the knee. Monday is your freshest, highest-quality run day; the long run Saturday is the week's key session. Keep them all easy (HR ≤138, conversational) — we build volume by adding time gradually, not by running harder.

🚴

Tue + Thu Group C — cross-training, not competition

The two Group C rides give you aerobic volume with zero running impact — ideal support for a run-focused week. The one rule: ride them easy (Zone 2, HR under ~140) and don't let the group turn them into a hard effort. Thursday's ride sits two days before your long run, so keeping it controlled protects Saturday. Sit in, spin 75+ rpm, and treat them as recovery-paced base miles.

🦶

Nudge the run cadence back toward 150

Your last two runs were at 141–143 spm — fine for easy reset runs, but as running becomes the focus, drift back toward 150. Quicker, lighter steps cut impact through the knee (your PFPS protection) and make Zone 2 pace feel smoother. Set the Garmin metronome and let it carry the rhythm — small, quick feet.

🩺

One check on the procedure timing

I've built this as a normal training week, assuming the procedure from your prep sheet isn't in this window. If it lands on any of these days, just tell me and I'll reshape the week around it — the prep day and procedure day become no-training days, with an easy return after. Don't train through prep or sedation.
