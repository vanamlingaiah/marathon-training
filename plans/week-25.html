The 6 AM window is your superpower now

In Aubrey this time of year, sunrise is around 6:20 AM and temps sit at 74–78°F from 6:15–7:00 AM before climbing fast. That's a genuine 45-minute Zone 2 window for outdoor running — enough for everything in this plan. Wake at 6:00, out the door by 6:15, finished before the heat. The whole plan is built around protecting that window.

Your new daily rhythm

Same shape every training day. Repetition is what turns a 6 AM alarm into an automatic habit. Lay everything out the night before.
