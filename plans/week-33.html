🚴 Making Two Rides Work — Load, Pace & Cadence

You asked if you can sustain 2 rides/week alongside running and strength. Short answer: yes — this structure does it safely. Here's the logic and the guardrails.

✓ Why it works
2 rides + 3 runs + 2 rest days. Rides land Tue & Sat with a rest day (Wed) or run days between. Bikes are zero-impact aerobic volume — great for your knee. Saturday's data proved the rides sit at easy effort, so they add base without deep fatigue.
🏃 Marathon stays #1
Running held at 3 days (Mon, Thu, Fri long). Strength trimmed to 1 full session this bike-heavy week — that's a fair in-season trade. If bikes ever start eating into run quality, runs win: Houston is the goal, the tri is the horizon.
🔧 Cadence is the knee key
Your ~54 rpm grind is the one thing to fix. 80–90 rpm in easier gears = less force per stroke = protected knees + fresher legs for running. Feels too easy at first; that's right. Watch it live on the Garmin.
⚠️ Before Tuesday — confirm the pace
Message the ride leader and ask what average speed "C Pace" holds. If it's ~12–13 mph, you'll be comfortable. If it's 15–16+, plan to ride your own effort and let the group go — do NOT redline to hang on. Also confirm: is it no-drop? what to carry for flats? start location? Your Saturday 9.8 mph avg was novice pace with heavy coasting — a steady C-pace group may be quicker, so go in with a self-sufficient, ride-your-own-race plan.
