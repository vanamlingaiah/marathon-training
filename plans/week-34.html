🔧 This week's #1 priority: the bike fit

Neck pain after every ride is a fit problem, not something to push through — and your plan to solve it is sound. The RS5's integrated cockpit sits low and the reach is long, forcing an aggressive forward lean that over-extends your neck. PlayTri's read is mechanically correct. And your triathlon logic is smart: the integrated cockpit won't accept aero bars, so switching to a standard stem + handlebar fixes the neck pain AND unlocks the future aero setup — two problems, one fix. Getting Jason's second opinion at Endure3 before spending is exactly right.

Ask for a fit-LED solution
Not just a parts swap. Ask Jason to measure you first (reach, saddle height, stack, flexibility) then spec the stem/bar length + angle to match. A new bar at the wrong length just moves the problem.
Mention the triathlon goal
Tell him you want a cockpit that will accept clip-on aero bars later. That way one change serves both the neck fix now and the tri position down the road.
🩹

Until the fit is fixed — ride neck-cautious

Rides this week stay in the ≤20-mile range and I've avoided two long back-to-back ride days, because duration is what triggers your neck. On the bike: relax your shoulders, bend your elbows (locked arms send every bump to the neck), shift hand positions often, and raise the front end if any spacer adjustment exists. After rides: gentle neck mobility (chin tucks, slow rotations). And keep lifting cadence — less weight on the hands eases the neck too.

🏊

Swim is now a fixture — Wednesday 9 AM

Two swims last week made it official: swimming is a real part of your training now. This week's session is locked for Wednesday 9 AM. Keep building what's working — you're up to ~950m and 45 min. Apply the coach's "one thing to practice," and remember the pool is your one cool, neck-friendly session (horizontal, no bar lean). Massage gun after: lats/delts/traps, lightest setting.

🏃

Two quality runs — your chosen rhythm

You asked for 2 good runs, and that's the right call in a bike-heavy, swim-added week. Both stay quality: one mid-week run-walk and the Friday long run (holding 70). Fewer run days means each one counts — go early, run gentle, keep cadence at 150. The marathon base stays protected even at 2 days because the long run is preserved.
