🥗 Nutrition — Event Week + The Endurance Ride

This is the most important part of the week. A 50-mile ride in extreme heat is won or lost on fueling and hydration. Here's the full plan — the week's build, race morning, and the exact on-bike schedule.

📅 The Week (Mon–Fri)
  • Hydration is cumulative — start Monday. Add an electrolyte drink daily; aim for pale-straw urine by Thursday.
  • Carb ramp from Wed — shift the plate toward rice, pasta, oats, potatoes, fruit. You're not "loading" hugely, just topping the tank over 3 days.
  • Protein stays steady (120–155g/day) for recovery — chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, fish.
  • Ease off very high fiber Thu–Fri to keep the gut calm on race day.
  • Sodium up as the week closes — you'll sweat heavily Saturday; prime the reservoir.
🌙 Day Before (Friday)
  • Carb-forward, moderate portions all day — top off, don't overstuff (overeating backfires).
  • Familiar foods only — nothing new or exotic the day before.
  • Hydrate with electrolytes steadily; carry a bottle and sip.
  • Dinner: pasta or rice + lean protein + easy-to-digest veg. Lower fiber, lower fat.
  • No alcohol. Early night — sleep matters more than any last workout.
🌅 Race Morning
  • Eat 2–2.5 hr before (up ~4:30, eat by 4:45–5:00 for a 7:05 start).
  • Familiar breakfast: oats + banana + honey + toast, or bagel + honey + a little nut butter.
  • 16–20 oz water + electrolyte with breakfast.
  • Top-up 30 min before: ½ banana or a few chews + sips of fluid.
  • Nothing new, nothing greasy or super high-fiber.
🏁 Post-Ride Recovery
  • Rehydrate aggressively — water + electrolytes; you'll finish depleted in the heat.
  • Protein + carbs within 30–45 min — recovery shake, then a real meal.
  • Keep sipping electrolytes for hours afterward.
  • Get out of the sun and cool down properly before the drive home.
  • Big meal + good sleep — the recovery is part of the event.
🧪 Scratch mixing rule — read first
One Scratch packet per bottle (20–22 oz) — never 2 in one bottle. Doubling the mix slows absorption, can upset your stomach, and leaves you short on fluid. So 2 packets = 2 bottles. Each Scratch ≈ 80 cal · 21g carb · 380mg sodium. Scratch is light on carbs for long rides, so you top up carbs with gels/bananas and sodium with a salt tab or aid-station snacks.

4-hour targets: ~160–240g carb · ~2,400–3,200mg sodium · ~80–112 oz fluid (4–5+ bottles). You cannot carry that fluid — refilling at every rest stop is mandatory, not optional. Carry ~4–5 Scratch packets, 5–6 gels, and a few salt tabs; use aid-station food to fill gaps.
📊 Tuesday vs Saturday — the key difference
Tuesday's 2 packets + 1 gel works for 2 hours. Saturday needs roughly double per hour and 4–5× the total fluid. Same habits (drink every 15 min, eat every 45), scaled way up — and the refills are what make it possible. Tuesday is your rehearsal for the routine; don't under-fuel Saturday by copying Tuesday's amounts.
⛔ STOP and get help if you feel any of these (heat illness)
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Nausea or headache
  • Goosebumps or chills in the heat
  • You stop sweating / skin goes dry
  • Muscle cramping
  • Confusion or feeling "off"

Any of these → get off the bike, into shade, to a rest stop or medical tent. Flag a SAG wagon. This is not weakness — heat stroke is a genuine emergency, and every rest stop has trained help. Finishing the shorter route or getting a ride back is always the right call over pushing into danger.
