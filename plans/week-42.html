Why we rebuild the foundation before piling on miles

You're motivated to run more — perfect. But more running + an achy knee + no strength work is exactly how the old injury cycle restarts. Your knee pain and back stiffness share one root: underactive glutes, hips and core. So the deal this week isn't "run as much as possible" — it's run consistently AND bring strength back, so the running is built on something solid. That's what lets the long run safely grow over the coming weeks toward marathon distance.

🏃 3 runs — easy, non-consecutive
Mon, Thu, Sat. All Zone 2 (≤136). Never two run days back-to-back while the knee is talking. Cadence 155+ — short, quick steps cut knee load.
💪 Strength — short, non-negotiable
Glute + core work that fixes knee AND back. Kept to 8–10 min and tacked onto runs so it actually happens. This is what makes the miles safe.
🦵

Your knee — let's fix the cause, and watch it closely

The niggle is almost certainly PFPS tied to form + weak glutes, not damage — and it's very fixable. Three levers: (1) Cadence 155+ — quicker, shorter steps keep your foot under you and cut patellar load 30–50%; (2) Glutes — the primer before every run + strength below, because strong glutes stop the knee caving in; (3) Warm up fully — cold muscles + a cold morning + an achy knee is a bad mix, so never skip the warm-up now that it's chilly. Ice 15 min after every run. And tell me: on a 0–10 scale, how much does it hurt, and when — during, after, going downstairs? That shapes how fast we build.

⏱️

The "no time for strength" fix — make it tiny and attached

You're skipping strength because there's no slot after your workout. So we stop treating it as a separate session: do 8–10 min of glute/core right at the end of your runs (you're already warm, no extra trip), and use "exercise snacks" at your standing desk during the workday — calf raises, glute squeezes, hip-flexor stretches every hour. That's your strength done with almost no extra time, and it directly targets the knee and back. Legs-up-the-wall in the evening drains the standing-day fatigue from your back.

🚴

Cranky Tuesday — your one ride, now in the evening

6:30 PM, C-pace, 29.7 mi, Frisco (flat-ish, 705 ft). Smart to move it to evenings since the mornings are cold now. It's your one intensity + social day — enjoy it, spin 80+. Two evening-specific notes: eat a proper carb lunch and a light snack ~1–1.5 hr before (banana + dates), and after a 9 PM finish, have your recovery meal but keep it moderate and give yourself a wind-down so the ride doesn't wreck your sleep. Wednesday is rest to absorb it.
