🚨 HR Sensor — Fix This Today (Before Week 22 Starts)

Three sessions with dropouts in the last two weeks: Wed W20 avg 96 · Wed W21 avg 96 · Fri W21 avg 62 (near resting rate). The Garmin HRM chest strap elastic is likely worn out or the electrode contacts are degraded. Every corrupted session means you can't validate Zone 2 compliance. Pick one fix below:

Option 1 ✅ Recommended
Replace the chest strap — Garmin HRM-Pro or HRM-Dual replacement strap <$30. Search "Garmin HRM strap replacement" on Amazon. The elastic wears out every 12–18 months with regular use. New strap = instant fix.
Option 2 immediate
Use Fenix 7 Pro optical HR — Go to Activity Settings → Heart Rate → Wrist Heart Rate. Less accurate than chest strap for intervals, but perfectly fine for Zone 2 bike and easy runs. Use this as a bridge until the new strap arrives.
Option 3 preventive
Electrode gel on strap contacts — Get Spectra 360 conductive gel ($8). Squeeze a pea-size on each electrode before wearing. Much more reliable than water in dry AC environments and prevents cold-start dropouts.

🌡️ Texas Heat — New Rules Start Now

Tuesday's run was in 75–84°F heat at 7:38 PM. That's your new normal through October. At 75°F+, heart rate is 8–12 bpm higher at the same pace — making Zone 2 compliance nearly impossible in the evening. All outdoor runs must move back to early morning.

Morning run: 5:30–7:00 AM
Temps 65–70°F, no direct sun. HR runs 5–8 bpm lower than evening. Zone 2 is achievable. Cadence holds better on cooler legs.
Evening run: 7:00–8:00 PM
Temps 78–86°F through summer. HR 8–12 bpm elevated = you'll cross 136 ceiling constantly. No benefit and higher injury risk. Avoid until October.
Zwift is heat-immune
Keep Zwift sessions any time — controlled environment. Run a fan directly at your face for sweat management and to prevent cardiac drift.
Hydration adjustment
Add 8–16 oz water per session from now on. Electrolytes mandatory before every outdoor run — sodium loss in Texas summer heat is significant.
