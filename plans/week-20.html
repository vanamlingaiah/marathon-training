Daily PT Anchor 5 min · every day · non-negotiable for PFPS + back

Stack this onto your morning coffee routine. Same time, same place, every day.

Glute Bridge
2 × 15 reps · 2 sec hold at top
Activates posterior chain that goes dormant during 8 hr standing days. Directly stabilizes lumbar spine.
Clamshells
2 × 15/side · band optional
Wakes up glute medius — the muscle whose weakness causes your knee to track inward (PFPS root cause).
Single-leg Balance
2 × 30 sec/side · eyes open
Trains foot/ankle proprioception. Cheap insurance against rolled ankles and improves running form.

Standing desk protocol (during work hours)

Calf raises every 45 min
15–20 reps
Activates venous pump — reduces afternoon leg fatigue that compounds next-morning training quality.
Lunch walk
10–15 min · outdoor if possible
Decompresses lumbar spine, resets posture, full venous flush. Skip this and Saturday's long run pays the price.
Legs up the wall · post-work
10–15 min · before dinner
Passively drains 8 hr of venous pooling. The single highest-ROI recovery tool you have.
