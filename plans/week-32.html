Your first group ride · 20 miles · ride YOUR race
The golden rule
Sit at the BACK, ride your own Zone 2, HR under ~140. Let faster riders go — novice rides regroup, you won't be dropped. If holding a wheel means redlining, let the gap open. Discipline > ego. This is a Z2 endurance ride, not a race.
Arrive 20 min early — meet the ride leader, say it's your first group ride and you're newer to cycling. Good groups look after novices.
Group skills: hold a steady predictable line, don't overlap wheels, point out hazards, call "car back/up," signal slowing/stopping. Don't brake suddenly.
Leave ~2 bike-lengths gap to the rider ahead until you're comfortable — no need to tuck in tight as a novice.
🍎 Pre-ride (90 min before)
Proper breakfast: oats + banana + honey + toast. 16 oz water + electrolyte. This is a longer effort than you've done outdoors — fuel for it.
🌡️ On the bike (20 mi in heat) ~1.5 hr
2 bottles with electrolyte (500–700mg sodium/hr). A gel or chews for mid-ride. It'll warm up fast after 8 AM — drink on schedule, not just when thirsty.
🦵 Knee watch
Longest ride yet = most pedal strokes yet. Any front-knee ache, back off effort and note saddle height for adjustment. Spin easy gears rather than grinding big ones.
🔫 Massage gun · post 6 min
Quads 90s → glutes 60s → calves 60s per side · hip flexors 30s LIGHT. Evening pass too if legs are cooked.
🍎 Post
Protein + carbs within 30 min, then a real meal. Rehydrate fully — long hot ride.

🚴 Group Ride — Prep, Etiquette & Safety

Everything for a smooth first group ride. Prep the kit Thursday/Friday so Saturday morning is calm. Read the club's "Group Terms and Signals" page beforehand.

🎒 Bring
2 full bottles (electrolyte) · spare tube + tire levers + pump/CO2 · phone + ID + a little cash · helmet (obviously) · sunglasses · sunscreen · small snack/gel. Ask the leader beforehand what novices are expected to carry for flats.
🤝 Etiquette
Hold a steady line — no sudden swerves or braking · point at hazards (holes, debris) · call "car back / car up" · signal slowing & stopping · don't overlap the wheel ahead · ride 2 abreast max and single up when told. Announce "on your left" when passing.
❤️ Pacing (your #1 job)
Zone 2, HR <140, sit at the back. Let surges go — novice rides regroup at turns/tops. If you'd have to redline to hold on, let the gap open. You're here for endurance + skills, not to race. Spin easy gears to protect the knee.
⚠️ Confirm before Saturday
Does the ride require STCC membership? Check the club site or email the ride leader · confirm the exact start location & time · ask if it's truly no-drop (most novice rides are) · tell them you're new to group riding so they can position you well. Skip headphones — you need to hear the group and traffic.
