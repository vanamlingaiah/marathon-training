🧘 The Stretch & Mobility Routine

Your dedicated focus this week. Dynamic moves before runs (to warm up), static holds after (to lengthen). Hold each static stretch 30 seconds per side, breathe into it, never bounce or force. Extra attention to hips and hip flexors — they tighten most from cycling.

🔥 Dynamic — before runs
  • Leg swings — front-to-back × 10/leg, side-to-side × 10/leg
  • Walking lunges × 8/leg — open the hips
  • High knees + butt kicks × 20 each
  • Hip circles × 10 each direction
  • Ankle rolls × 10 each
  • 3–4 easy strides at cadence 150 to finish
🧊 Static — after runs / rest days
  • Hip flexor lunge stretch 30s/side — key after cycling
  • Pigeon pose 45s/side — deep glute/hip
  • Standing quad stretch 30s/side
  • Hamstring stretch (seated or standing) 30s/side
  • Calf stretch (straight + bent knee) 30s/side each
  • Figure-4 glute stretch 30s/side
🎯 Cycling-recovery extras
  • Doorway chest stretch 30s — opens the front after bent-over riding
  • Upper-trap + neck stretch 30s/side — releases ride/desk tension
  • Cat-cow × 10 — mobilizes the spine
  • Thoracic rotations × 8/side — upper-back mobility
  • Couch stretch 45s/side — deep hip flexor (go easy)
🧵 Foam roll (Fri + as needed)
  • Quads — slow passes, 30s/leg
  • IT band — gentle, 30s/side
  • Calves — 30s/leg
  • Glutes — 30s/side
  • Upper back — 30s (never lower back)
